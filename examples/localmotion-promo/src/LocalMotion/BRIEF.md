# Brief (verbatim)

Make a 45-second horizontal (16:9) promo video for "localmotion", an open-source project. Fast, confident, tech style: real animation, not slides.

Content to use exactly:
- Hook: "What if your videos cost 0 tokens?"
- Then: "localmotion: motion-design videos made by an open model on your own GPU."
- How it works, as four quick beats: "Write a brief." / "The model writes the video as code." / "It checks its work and looks at every frame." / "The finished video lands on your phone."
- Big numbers: "0 tokens" · "0 API bills" · "1 GPU"
- Proof: show the sample videos from the material folder (a racing-game trailer, a sandbox-game pitch, a lighthouse short film) as if playing in floating device frames: phone frames for the vertical ones, a monitor frame for the horizontal one.
- Ending: "localmotion" then "Open source. Your GPU, your videos."

Visual ideas (adapt them, invent the rest): a brief being typed in a terminal, lines of code flowing into a video frame, a token counter that stays stuck at 0, a GPU chip pulsing with light, a chat notification "Your video is ready" sliding in on a phone (generic chat bubble, no real app logos), glitch or wipe transitions between beats.

Material for this video: public/localmotion-promo

## Decisions
- 1920x1080, 30 fps, exactly 1350 frames (45 s).
- Style: tech type pair, deep near-black background, cool white text, one terminal-green accent.
- Transitions: hard cuts with green flash + glitch slice bursts.
